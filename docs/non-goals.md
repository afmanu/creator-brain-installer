# Non-goals

Creator Brain Installer does not:

- schedule or publish content;
- manage editorial queues, tasks, campaigns or production status;
- store every post, note, conversation or source document;
- continuously scrape social networks;
- authenticate to an account without a separate explicit action;
- optimize for an undocumented platform algorithm;
- provide autonomous background memory;
- require a specific model, vendor or application;
- replace AI Context Installer or Content OS;
- silently synchronize profile changes between systems.

Content operations belong in Content OS. General AI collaboration preferences belong in AI Context Installer when the user chooses to use it. Creator Brain remains usable without either product.
